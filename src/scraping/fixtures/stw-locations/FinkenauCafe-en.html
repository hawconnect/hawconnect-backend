Café Finkenau

At Café Finkenau, students and employees on the Finkenau Art and Media Campus can enjoy hot and cold coffee specialities, beer (from 3 p.m.), smoothies and delicious cakes and snacks. All offers are of course also available ‘to go’. A modern ambience, a direct view of the Eilbek Canal and a spacious terrace invite you to learn and relax. All sandwiches are sold at half price during happy hour, which starts 15 minutes before closing time. 


The café is wheelchair-friendly.
