Cafeteria Berliner Tor

The Berliner Tor cafeteria is located on the first floor in the high-rise building of the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Applied Sciences (HAW).
Discover the culinary variety with delicious dishes from the wok station, from the grill in the front cooking area or freshly baked pizza from the pizza point. During happy hour, you can get all self-service meals at half price 15 minutes before the food counter closes (salad bar, pasta bar, vegetable bar, desserts, soups).

Cafeteria Berliner Tor is barrier-free. Tray trolleys are available for wheelchair users and high chairs for the youngest guests.
