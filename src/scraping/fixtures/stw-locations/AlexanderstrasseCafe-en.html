Café Alexanderstraße

Café Alexanderstraße is located in the immediate vicinity of Lohmühlenstraße station and Berliner Tor and offers you a great ambience with its lounge character. We offer an extensive breakfast menu, hot meals & snacks, bagels & baguettes, donuts & muffins, coffee & tea specialities, cakes & tarts and soft drinks. During our happy hour, you can get all sandwiches for half price 15 minutes before closing time. This café is also barrier-free and highchairs are available for our youngest guests.
