Cafeteria Bergedorf

The cafeteria is located on the campus of the Faculty of Life Sciences at the University of Applied Sciences (HAW) in Bergedorf.
In the cafeteria Bergedorf you will not only find our front cooking area, but also our large salad buffet and the pasta or vegetable bar. You can get all self-service meals (salad bar, pasta bar, vegetable bar, desserts, soups) for half price 15 minutes before the food counter closes during happy hour.

In good weather, the spacious terrace with modern wooden loungers and comfortable seating area invites you to eat and relax outdoors. The cafeteria is barrier-free. Tray trolleys are available for wheelchair users. The canteen also has a children's play area and highchairs.

Pay cashless with debit or credit card, cash payment is not possible.
