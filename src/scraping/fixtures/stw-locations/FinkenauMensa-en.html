Cafeteria Finkenau

Since 2015, the cafeteria on the Finkenau art and media campus has offered a varied and delicious range of meat, vegetarian and vegan dishes in a modern setting. You can customise your meal at the pasta, salad and vegetable bar. There is also a front cooking area where you can watch your food being freshly prepared in a wok or on the grill, for example. For those with a sweet tooth, there is a colourful selection of delicious desserts. Happy hour starts 15 minutes before the food counter closes, during which you can get all self-service meals at half price (salad bar, pasta bar, vegetable bar, desserts, soups).

Mensa Finkenau is barrier-free and highchairs are available for the youngest guests.
